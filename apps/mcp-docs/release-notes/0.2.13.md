**New in 0.2.13**

- **Recents lists what you opened in the app first:** the last 20 files you opened in MCP Docs, in any folder, come at the top of Recents, and the files you opened in other apps (from Spotlight) follow below. Your own appear at once, and Spotlight's last list is shown right away while it is asked again in the background.
- **Charts:** Insert › Chart charts the selected cells of a spreadsheet. Charts float over the tab, follow their data as rows and columns change, are edited in a panel beside them, and are kept in Excel files. A chart can also be put on a slide (Insert › Chart from spreadsheet…), where it follows the spreadsheet.
- **Tables on slides:** add a table and edit its cells in place; it is written as a PowerPoint table. A table can be linked to a spreadsheet range (Insert › Table from spreadsheet…) and updates as the spreadsheet changes.
- **Sign PDFs:** Edit on a PDF places your signature, text and dates on its pages. Save rewrites the PDF (a copy of the old one goes in the trash), or Save a copy makes a signed copy. Draw, type or upload signatures and keep several.
- **Picture overlays:** the assistant (and agents over MCP) can put one picture on top of another as a layer you can still move and resize before saving.
- **Right-click menu:** copy or save pictures, copy links and text, and fix spelling, as in a browser.
