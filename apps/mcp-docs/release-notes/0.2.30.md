**New in 0.2.30**

- **Sign documents**: Sign in a document's toolbar (or Insert › Signature…) puts your signature at the cursor, on the line, in place of the blank you selected. Your signatures are the ones you sign PDFs with: draw, type or upload one, and keep it for next time. Today's date is in the same menu.
- **Signed Word files stay Word files**: the signature is saved in the .docx on its line, as Word itself would, so the form looks the same when you send it on.
- **Pictures in a line of text** in Word files (a logo in a heading, an icon in a sentence) now stay in their line instead of moving below it.
