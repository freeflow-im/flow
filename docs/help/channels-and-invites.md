# Channels & Invites

## Channels

A channel is a named conversation about one topic. Public channels can be found
and joined by anyone in the workspace; private channels are visible only to the
people who have been added to them.

- **Post** in the composer at the bottom. Markdown works: `**bold**`, lists, links, and fenced code blocks.
- **Thread** any message to reply without interrupting the main flow. Threads keep their own unread count.
- **React** with an emoji when a reply would be noise.
- **Mention** someone with `@` to notify them — that works for agents too.

## Direct messages

A DM is a private conversation with one other member. Agents can be DM'd exactly
like people, which is the usual way to hand one a task on your own.

## Invites

Admins can bring new people in two ways:

1. **An invite link** — a shareable URL that anyone can use to join the workspace. Links can be revoked when you no longer want them circulating.
2. **An email invite** — sent to one address, usable once.

Once someone has joined the workspace, adding them to a channel is separate:
open the channel's member list and add them there.

## Visitors

A **visitor** is someone with no account whom you let into one public channel by
a link — a customer, a guest speaker, someone you are working with for a day.

- **Invite a visitor** from the channel's **⋯** menu. You get a link to copy; it
  works for 24 hours. The same dialog lists the channel's open links, who made
  each and how many people came in by it, with **Turn off** (which also removes
  everyone who came in by that link).
- The visitor opens the link, types their name and is in. They see only the
  messages posted **after they joined**, can post, reply in threads, react and
  share files, and can mention the channel's people (not agents). They cannot
  see anything else in the organization.
- A visitor's name carries a **Visitor** label. The channel's people list has a
  **Visitors** section, where any member can **Remove** one.
- A visitor stays signed in to that channel in their browser, and their access
  ends 30 days after their last visit, when they press **Leave**, or when a
  member removes them.
- Visitor links are only for public channels, and guest accounts cannot make
  them. Owners and admins can turn visitors off for the whole organization in
  **Admin → General**, "Visitors in channels".
