# Huddles

A huddle is a quick video call with the people in a conversation. It runs in
**Meet**, FreeFlow's meeting app, so a huddle has everything a meeting has:
video, screen sharing, and a link you can send.

Huddles are for small rooms: a DM, a group DM, or a channel with **six people
or fewer** (AI agents do not count). In a bigger channel there is no Huddle
button; start a meeting from Meet and post its link instead.

## Starting and joining one

The huddle button sits in the header of the conversation, next to its name.

- **Huddle** starts one. Everyone in the conversation who has FreeFlow open is
  rung: they see a card with **Join** and **Decline**. If someone cannot be
  reached you are told, and a DM gets a "Missed huddle", "Call declined" or
  "Call ended" line afterwards.
- The call opens in **a new browser tab**. The conversation stays where it was
  in the first tab, so you can keep reading and writing while you talk.
- **Join Huddle** shows when a huddle is already running, with a count of the
  people in it. Pressing it opens the same call.
- **Back to Huddle** shows while you are in it, and takes you to its tab.

There is one huddle per conversation, and it keeps the same link, so a huddle
link you copied earlier still works. Archived channels cannot host a huddle.

## In the call

You join with your **microphone on and your camera off**. The bar at the
bottom has:

- **Mute / Unmute** — your microphone.
- **Camera** — turn your camera on or off.
- **Share screen** — the browser asks which tab, window or screen to share.
  A shared screen is shown large, with everyone in a strip below it.
- **People** — who is in the call.
- **Leave** — leave the call. Closing the tab leaves too.

**Copy link** at the top copies the call's link, to send to someone else in
your organization. Nothing is recorded.

## Microphone and camera permissions

The first time you join, your browser asks whether FreeFlow may use the
microphone, and again the first time you turn the camera on. The browser
remembers your answer for the site. If you blocked it, use the camera or
padlock icon in the address bar to allow it again, then reload the call.

## Where huddles work

Huddles run in Meet on the **web** and in the **desktop app**. On the **Mac**
and **iPhone**, the Huddle button and the ring card open the call in your
browser; a native call screen comes later.

Worth knowing:

- The incoming-call card only appears while FreeFlow is open on that device;
  there is no push notification that rings a sleeping phone yet. Answering on
  one device dismisses the card on your others.
- AI agents do not join huddles by voice. Voice calls with agents were removed;
  an agent can still take part in the conversation itself.
- Huddles need Meet to be set up on the server your workspace runs on. If the
  huddle button says it cannot start, ask whoever runs your workspace.
