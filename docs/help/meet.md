# Meet

**Meet** is FreeFlow's app for video meetings. Open it from the bar at the top
(or press ⌘5).

## Starting a meeting

- **Start a meeting now** makes a meeting and opens it in a new tab.
- **Copy a link for later** makes a meeting and copies its link, to paste into
  a message, an email or a calendar event.
- **Live now** lists your organization's meetings that are running, with who
  is in each. Press **Join** to go in.

A meeting's link looks like `…/meet/kqd-wmzt-fra`. It keeps working, so you can
use the same link for a meeting that repeats.

## Joining

Opening a link shows a check screen first: your camera, buttons to turn the
microphone and camera on or off, and who is already in. Press **Join now**.

A huddle you start or answer in Chat skips this screen and goes straight in.

## Guests

Anyone with a meeting's link can join, even without a FreeFlow account. A guest
types their name on the check screen and goes straight in; there is no waiting
room. Guests show as **Guest** in the People list.

- If you are in the organization, sign in first (the check screen has a
  **Sign in** link) so you join as yourself.
- Huddles are only for the people in their conversation, so guests cannot join
  a huddle.

## Locking a meeting

The person who made a meeting can press **Lock** at the top of the meeting.
While it is locked, no new guests can join; people in your organization still
can. Press it again to unlock.

## In the meeting

The bar at the bottom has **Mute**, **Camera**, **Share screen**, **People**
and **Leave**. **Copy link** at the top copies the meeting's link. Nothing is
recorded.
