# Meet: the side panel's width can be dragged

- [web] Meet's side panel (Chat, People) has a drag handle on its left edge: 280–760 px, kept in this browser, double-click to reset.

## Feature

- **Make the meeting chat wider.** Drag the left edge of Meet's Chat or People panel to make it wider or narrower. Meet remembers the width; double-click the edge to go back.
