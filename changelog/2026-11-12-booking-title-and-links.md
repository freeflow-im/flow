# Booking page: a title without a length, and the guest's links in the invitation

- `[server]` Default title "Meeting"; a saved old default ("30 Minute Meeting") reads as "Meeting" where the page offers other lengths.
- `[server]` The booked event's description holds the guest's note and their cancel/reschedule link (the invitation shows it), not a line written to the owner. The platform's own confirmation mail goes only when no invitation was sent.

## Feature

- **Clearer booking invitations.** A guest's invitation now has their link to cancel or reschedule, and a booking page's title no longer names one meeting length.
