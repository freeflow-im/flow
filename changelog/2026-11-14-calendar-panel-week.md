# Calendar side panel: Week view

- `[web]` The Calendar side panel has Day | Week: Week lists the seven days with each day's events as cards tinted with their calendar's colour (all-day ones in the colour), "Nothing scheduled" on an empty day. The choice is remembered; titles are short ("Nov 8 – 14").

## Feature

- **See your week beside your mail.** The Calendar tab in Mail's side panel now has a Week view: each day of the week with its events, so you can see what is coming up while you read and write email.
