# Slides: outdented bullets keep their level after editing

- [web] [desktop] A level-0 bullet after an indented one no longer turns indented when the text box loses focus. Paragraphs now always carry `data-level` and `data-bullet`, so the editor does not copy the line above.

## Feature

- **Bullet indents stay put in Slides.** Indenting some bullets no longer pushes the bullets below them in by one level when you click out of the text box.
