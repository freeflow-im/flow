# Suite bar: another app opens in its own browser tab

- [web] Choosing another app in the suite bar (or ⌘1–9) opens it in a browser tab named for that app, so choosing it again returns to that tab. The desktop and native apps still switch in place.

## Feature

Choosing another app at the top of the page (for example Mail while on Home) opens it in its own browser tab. Choose it again and you go back to that same tab.
