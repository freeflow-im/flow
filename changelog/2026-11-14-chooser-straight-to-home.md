# Choosing a workspace opens Home without passing through Chat

- `[web]` The workspace chooser stores the choice and goes to Home, showing "Opening …", instead of selecting the workspace in Chat first, which mounted Chat for a moment before Home loaded. The desktop app (Chat only) still opens it in Chat.

## Feature

- **Straight to Home.** After you sign in and pick a workspace, Home opens directly, with no brief stop in Chat on the way.
