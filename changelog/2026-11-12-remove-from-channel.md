# Chat: Remove from channel in the members list

- `[web]` The channel members popover has Remove (asked once more as "Remove?") on the rows the server allows: anyone for an owner or admin, and your own assistant for you. The server's refusal shows in the popover.
- `[macos]` `[ios]` Not yet: recorded in the Parity section.

## Feature

- **Take someone out of a channel from its members list.** Open the members list in a channel's header and choose Remove next to a person, then confirm. Owners and admins can remove anyone; everyone can remove their own assistant.
