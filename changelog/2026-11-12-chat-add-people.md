# Chat: Add people from the channel header

- [web] The channel header's member list has "Add people", which opens a dialog to add organization members to the channel. Inside Home, Chat hides its channel list, and that list's row menu was the only way to invite.
- [web] The list offers your own assistant and shared agents, but not other people's assistants (the server refuses those).

## Feature

You can add people and agents to a channel again from inside Home: click the member avatars at the top right of a channel, then "Add people".
