# Whiteboard: pasted pictures show at once

- [web] [desktop] A pasted or uploaded picture now shows from the browser's copy right away; it uploads behind it and takes its stored address in the board and its undo steps when done (`DeckStore.rewriteElements`). Before, it waited for the upload and then a second download for its size.

## Feature

Pictures you paste or upload onto a whiteboard now appear right away, instead of after a second or more.
