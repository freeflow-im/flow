# Mail: images asked about once, and messages in place of the list

- `[web]` Mail hides a message's images from the web only until you choose "Show images" once; from then on every message shows them. Account settings has the switch to ask again (kept in this browser).
- `[web]` Mail opens a message in place of the list, as Gmail does, with a back arrow, "3 of 50" and newer/older arrows; j and k open the older and newer message, Escape goes back. The button beside Refresh puts messages beside the list instead (kept in this browser). Narrow screens always show them in place of the list.
- `[web]` With messages in place of the list, the list is Gmail's: one line a message (sender, bold subject when unread - snippet, time or date), read messages on a tinted row, and Archive, Delete and Mark read/unread on hover.
