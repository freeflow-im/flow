# Mail: email links in a message open a new message in Mail

- `[web]` A click on a `mailto:` link in a message body (HTML or plain text) opens Mail's composer with its to, cc, bcc,
  subject and body, instead of the browser's mail handler.

## Feature

- **Email addresses in a message open a new message.** Click an email address or "email us" link in a message and
  Mail opens a new message to that person, with any subject or cc the link includes.
