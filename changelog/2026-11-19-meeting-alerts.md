# Meeting alerts under the suite bar

- `[web]` `[desktop]` The shared suite bar can show alerts: cards that slide down below the account menu, each closed with ×. A page can add its own through the `alerts` prop.
- `[web]` `[desktop]` The first kind is a meeting alert. It covers accepted timed meetings, or ones you organized with guests or a call, from 2 minutes before the start until you close it or 5 minutes past the start. It has Join for the video link and Open in Calendar. Closing it closes it in every tab.

## Feature

- **Meeting reminders, wherever you are in FreeFlow.** Two minutes before a meeting you accepted, a card slides down from the top right of any app, with a Join button for the video call. It goes away when you close it or once the meeting has been going for five minutes.
