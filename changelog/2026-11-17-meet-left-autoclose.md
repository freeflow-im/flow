# Meet: the tab closes itself after you leave

- [web] "You left the meeting." counts down 30 s and closes the tab (Keep it open stops it). When the browser will not close the tab, a member goes to Meet's home and a guest is told the tab can be closed.

## Feature

- **Meeting tabs tidy themselves up.** After you leave a meeting, its tab closes by itself after 30 seconds, with a small countdown. Click **Keep it open** to stop it.
