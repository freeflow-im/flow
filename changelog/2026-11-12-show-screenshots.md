# The assistant's browser screenshots show in the chat

- `[web]` A browser screenshot the assistant takes shows under its "Browser screenshot" line in Docs', Mail's and Calendar's panels (click for full size), live and after a reload; until now only the model saw it.
- `[server]` The transcript puts each step's screenshot on its tool line and keeps the app's "attached above" note out of the person's messages (`toChatItems`).

## Feature

- When your assistant takes a screenshot of a web page, you see the picture in the chat too.
