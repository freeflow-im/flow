# A personal agent reaches its own calendar

- `[server]` A personal agent's calendar tools now reach its own FreeFlow calendar beside its sponsor's: `list_calendars` lists it marked `yours`, and a request naming it goes to the agent's Calendar object, as the agent. Same in the panel, Chat, wake jobs and Notes turns.
- `[server]` Its prompt now says it has a calendar ("your calendar" vs "my calendar"); before, it told its sponsor it had none.

## Feature

- **Your assistant can see its own calendar.** Ask it about "your meetings" and it reads the meetings it was invited to, beside your own calendar. It can also put events on its calendar and answer its invitations.
