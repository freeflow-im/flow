# Mail: Gmail-style inbox tabs (Primary, Promotions, Social, Updates, Forums)

- `[edge]` Inbox tabs per account (`MailAccount.tabs`, set with `PATCH /v1/mail/accounts/:id { tabs }`, no re-verification). Gmail accounts use Gmail's own categories through `X-GM-RAW` searches; other servers are sorted by header rules in `src/imap/categorize.ts` (List-Id, List-Unsubscribe, List-Post, Precedence, Auto-Submitted, Feedback-ID, ESP and GitHub/Google Groups headers, sender domain), cached per `(account, folder, uidValidity, uid)` in the Mail object's SQLite.
- `[edge]` `GET .../messages?category=` lists one tab (newest first, at most 300 headers fetched per request, with `next` to carry on); summaries carry `category` in INBOX when tabs are on; `GET .../categories` gives each tab's unread, new-since and latest message; `POST .../messages/category` moves messages' senders to a tab (kept per sender, ours only, Gmail's labels untouched) and `POST .../senders/category` undoes it.
- `[web]` Mail shows the tab bar above the inbox list (icons, blue underline, coloured "N new" pills with the latest sender and subject; icons and counts when narrow), keeps the tab in the address (`&tab=`), Account settings has an Inbox tabs section, and "Move to tab" is in each row's menu (dense list), the bulk actions and the message's Move menu, with Undo.

## Feature

- **Inbox tabs, as in Gmail.** Turn on Promotions, Social, Updates and Forums in Account settings, each by itself, and FreeFlow Mail sorts your inbox into them; everything else, mainly mail from people, stays in Primary. Each tab shows how much new mail has come in since you last looked, and who sent the latest. Gmail accounts use Gmail's own sorting.
- **Move a message to another tab.** From a message's menu, or with several selected, choose Move to tab: that sender's mail goes there from now on, and Undo puts it back.
