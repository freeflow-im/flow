# Slides: sticky notes, as on whiteboards

- `[web]` Slides toolbar gets a Sticky note button and the Note color menu; the note is the whiteboard's (a text box
  with a `fill`).
- `[web]` The assistant's `edit_elements` takes `fill` on text elements, and `read_deck` reports it.
- `[web]` PowerPoint export keeps a note's paper color behind its text.

## Feature

- **Sticky notes in Slides.** Add a sticky note to a slide from the toolbar, type on it, and pick its color, just like
  on a whiteboard. The assistant can add them too, and they keep their color when you export to PowerPoint.
