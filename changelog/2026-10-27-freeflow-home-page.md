# freeflow.im becomes the Freeflow home page; the Flow page moves to flow.freeflow.im

- [web] New `freeflowhome/` site for `freeflow.im`: lists Flow, MCP Docs, Skill Explorer and Browser Session Share. Deployed by `deploy-home.yml` to the Pages project `freeflow-home`.
- [web] `flowlandingpage/` now targets `flow.freeflow.im`: nav gains Log in next to Sign up, links back to the home page, and GitHub links use `freeflow-im/flow`.
- [web] `app.freeflow.im/?register` opens the Register form; every Sign up button uses it.

## Feature

- **One home for every Freeflow tool.** freeflow.im now lists all of our free, open-source tools. The Flow page, with Log in and Sign up, is at flow.freeflow.im.
- **Sign up goes straight to the sign-up form.** The Sign up buttons now open the Register form instead of the Sign in form.
