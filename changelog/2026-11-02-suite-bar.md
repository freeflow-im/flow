# The FreeFlow suite bar on Chat and Docs

- `[web]` The suite bar (`packages/suite-bar`, shared by both clients) above Chat and the Workspace app (Docs): organization menu, Docs | Chat | Mail | Calendar, search (⌘K), Assistant, notifications, account menu; ⌘1–⌘4 switch apps.
- `[web]` Chat: the bar replaces the workspace rail and the sidebar's status footer; Help moved to the account menu; `/?open=chooser|admin|invite|activity|profile` for links from Docs.
- `[web]` Docs in the suite: the bar replaces the logo, Sign out and the Assistant button in page headers; standalone and desktop are unchanged.
- `[web]` Chat's violet is the suite's blue (`#1A73E8`), the default sidebar navy, and the sign-in screen shows the FreeFlow mark.

## Feature

- **One bar across FreeFlow.** A bar at the top of Chat and Docs switches between the two apps, switches organizations, searches (⌘K), opens the Assistant and holds your account: status, away, profile, settings and log out. Chat is blue now, like Docs.
