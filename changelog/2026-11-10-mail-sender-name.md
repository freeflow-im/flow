# Mail: a conversation with one writer names them in full

- `[web]` The list showed only the first word of a sender's name ("The" for The New York Times): the first-names rule meant for several writers applied to one too. One writer is now named in full; several keep their first names.

## Feature

- In Mail's message list, a sender like "The New York Times" or "Liam Carter" shows with their full name; conversations with several people still read "Bob, me, Chen".
