# Apps opens inside Home

- `[web]` Home's Apps row opens the Apps page in Home's frame, as Mail, Docs and the others do, instead of leaving Home.
- `[web]` Inside Home, the Apps page sends its menu (the installed apps, then Build an app) and its address to Home's sidebar.
- `[web]` A link to `/apps/…` inside another framed app (Admin's Open) now opens in Home too.

## Feature

- **Apps stays inside Home.** Opening Apps from Home's sidebar now shows it beside the sidebar, like Mail and Docs, with your installed apps and Build an app listed under the Apps row.
