# The assistant knows and reads its own mailbox

- `[server]` The assistant's prompt names its own address and who may mail it; a shared agent's names its address too.
- `[server]` In panel and Chat turns, the assistant's mail tools list its own hosted mailbox beside the person's accounts (marked `yours`) and read it from the agent's own Mail object, as the agent.

## Feature

- **Your assistant knows its own inbox.** Ask Ada "what's in your inbox?" and she reads the mail sent to her own address, not only yours.
