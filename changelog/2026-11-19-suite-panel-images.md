# Images in the suite's assistant panel

- `[web]` `[desktop]` The shared assistant panel (Build app page, Home, Admin, installed apps) takes images: ⌘V paste, drag-and-drop or the attach button. Images are downscaled in the browser as in Docs, with up to 4 thumbnails per message, and sent as the turn's `images`.

## Feature

- **Paste screenshots into the assistant everywhere.** On the Build app page, Home, Organization settings and over installed apps, you can paste a screenshot with ⌘V, drop an image, or attach one, and the assistant sees it with your message.
