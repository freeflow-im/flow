# Home: smaller app menus

- [web] Home's app menus hold only the key items: Mail has New message, Inbox (an account list when there are several) and Drafts; Docs has New document and its three lists; Calendar has New event and Today.

## Feature

The menus under Mail, Docs and Calendar in Home's sidebar are now short: a "New" item and the two or three places you use most.
