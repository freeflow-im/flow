# Alert cards slide in and out

- `[web]` Suite-bar alert cards slide down into view from above the window's top edge, and slide up and out when closed here, closed in another tab, or ended. A leaving card keeps its place in the stack; less motion (`prefers-reduced-motion`) shows and removes them at once.

## Feature

- **Alerts slide in and out.** An alert card now slides down from the top of the window, and slides back up out of sight when you close it.
