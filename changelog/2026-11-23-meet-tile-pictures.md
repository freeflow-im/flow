# Meet: tiles and People draw participants' pictures

- `[web]` The Meet room drew initials for everyone with no video and never read RealtimeKit's `picture`; tiles and the
  People list now show it, with initials when there is none or it fails to load.

## Feature

- **See faces in meetings.** When someone's camera is off, their tile shows their profile picture instead of initials,
  and agents you send show theirs.
