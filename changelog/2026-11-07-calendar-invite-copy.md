# Calendar: your own invitation copy wins over a shared calendar's; agents marked in the guest list (platform phase 4.5)

- [web] Events merged by UID for the grid: the person's own copy (with Yes / No / Maybe) hides a shared calendar's copy of the same event, and between two shared calendars the first wins; the card says "Also on Ann's calendar".
- [web] Guest list marks agents "agent" instead of "member".

## Feature

An invitation you can see on your own calendar and on a colleague's shared calendar now shows once, as your copy you can answer, and agents in a guest list are marked as agents.
