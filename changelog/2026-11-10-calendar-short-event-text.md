# Calendar: readable titles on short events

- `[web]` Time-grid events of 15 minutes or less get smaller title text with no vertical padding, and any short event's title a tighter line height, so the title fits the box instead of being cut off.

## Feature

- **Short events show their title.** A 15-minute event on the calendar now uses smaller text, so you can read what it is instead of seeing the top half of the words.
