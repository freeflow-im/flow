# Mail: a person card on names and addresses

- `[web]` Hovering (or focusing) a name or address in an open message — the sender, the "to" line, Details — shows a
  card: name, address with a copy button, Send mail, Find emails, Open in / Add to Contacts, and Message in Chat for
  members of the organization.
- `[web]` A new message opened with recipients (the card's Send mail) closes without asking while nothing is written.

## Feature

- **Person card in Mail.** Hover over someone's name or email address in a message to see who they are, copy their
  address with one click, write to them, find their emails, add them to Contacts, or message them in Chat if they are
  in your organization.
