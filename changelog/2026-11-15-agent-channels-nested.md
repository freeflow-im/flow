# Channels an agent made sit under the agent in the sidebar

- `[web]` `[desktop]` Chat's sidebar lists a channel an agent created (with its sub-channels) indented under that agent in Agents, not in Channels; a sub-channel an agent made in a person's channel stays with its parent.
- `[web]` Home's sidebar does the same under each agent row.
- `[web]` `[desktop]` A chevron on the agent row folds its channel list (remembered per device, shared by Home and Chat); a dot shows when a folded channel needs you.

## Feature

- **Agent channels stay with their agent.** When an agent opens a channel for its work and invites you, the channel now shows indented under that agent in the sidebar of Home and Chat, so your Channels list stays short. Click the arrow on the agent's row to fold its channels away.
