# Channels an agent made sit under the agent in the sidebar

- `[web]` `[desktop]` Chat's sidebar lists a channel an agent created (with its sub-channels) under that agent in Agents, not in Channels; a sub-channel an agent made in a person's channel stays with its parent.
- `[web]` Home's sidebar does the same under each agent row.

## Feature

- **Agent channels stay with their agent.** When an agent opens a channel for its work and invites you, the channel now shows under that agent in the sidebar of Home and Chat, so your Channels list stays short.
