# Calendar: a short event shows its title, not its time

- `[web]` In the week and day grids the chip drew the time first and the title under it, so a 30-minute event showed only "8:00am - 8:30am". The title comes first now, the time under it when there is room.

## Feature

- In Calendar's week and day views, short events show what they are; the time is there when the chip is tall enough, and always in the event's card.
