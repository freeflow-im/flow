# Contacts: the fifth app, added by manifest (platform phase 6.5)

- `[server]` A `Contacts` object per account (`src/contacts`, migration v8): own contacts, an `org` viewer grant to share them, `contacts.changed` events; `/v1/contacts` merges own, members, shared lists and people written to from Mail.
- `[server]` Tools `list_contacts`, `find_contact`, `add_contact` at `/mcp` and in the assistant under a new Contacts consent group (`contacts:read`, `contacts:write`).
- `[server]` Kernel gaps closed so a manifest covers an app: app objects reached by binding name (`src/apps.ts`) for tool contexts at both doors and for references; kinds declare `address`, `icon`, `label`; generic tool-definition writing; hashed assets for every client path.
- `[web]` FreeFlow Contacts at `/contacts` (`packages/contacts`): search, grouped list, cards with DM/copy chips, add/edit/delete, share toggle; a Contacts tab (⌘5) in every suite bar, whose shortcuts now run past ⌘4.

## Feature

FreeFlow Contacts, a new app in the suite bar (⌘5): your own contacts, everyone in your organization and the people you have emailed, in one searchable list. Click a colleague's address to message them in Chat, add and edit your own contacts, and share your contacts with your organization with one switch. Agents you connect can look people up and add contacts too.
