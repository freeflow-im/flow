# FreeFlow Home at /home

- `[web]` `[desktop]` New `packages/home` (docs/specs/home.md), added by its manifest with no object or routes: a sidebar of the apps, channels and agents, and Today, Inbox, Recent files and Mentions cards; every row opens its app's own page.
- `[web]` Empty cards name the next step (open Calendar, set up Mail, create a doc, invite the team, add an agent): the start of a setup flow.
- `[web]` `[desktop]` The suite bar's Home tab comes first; ⌘ digits now follow each tab's manifest shortcut, so ⌘1–⌘4 are unchanged.

## Feature

- **A home page for the whole suite.** Home shows today's meetings, your latest mail, your recent files and your mentions in one place, with your apps, channels and agents down the side. Click anything to open it in its app.
- **A clear next step.** On a new organization, each empty card tells you what to do first, such as connecting a calendar or inviting your team.
