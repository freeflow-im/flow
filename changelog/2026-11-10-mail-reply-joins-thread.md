# Mail: a sent reply appears in the open conversation

- `[web]` After a reply was sent, the list and the folders were refreshed but not the open conversation, so the reply showed only after reopening it. The conversation is refreshed too.
- `[server]` The send brings the Sent folder's cache up to date before it answers, so the conversation read right after a send already holds the copy.

## Feature

- In Mail, a reply you send appears at the bottom of the conversation you are reading, without reopening it.
