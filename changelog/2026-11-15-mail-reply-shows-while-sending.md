# Mail: a reply shows in its conversation as you press Send

- [web] An answer is added to the open conversation at once, marked "Sending…", until the server's copy (matched by Message-ID) replaces it; a failed send removes it.
- [web] A conversation that gains a message keeps its open and closed messages as they were, instead of collapsing.

## Feature

When you reply to an email, your reply appears in the conversation the moment you press Send, and the conversation stays open the way you had it.
