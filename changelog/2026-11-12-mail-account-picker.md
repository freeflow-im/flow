# Mail's account picker moves to the Mail row in Home's sidebar

- `[web]` With several mail accounts, Home's sidebar shows `Mail <domain> ▾` (the current account); it lists the accounts. Inbox no longer opens an account list. The menu protocol gains an optional `picker` for an app's row.

## Feature

- **Switch mail accounts from the Mail row.** In Home's sidebar, the Mail row shows the current account's domain; click it to pick which account's mail to show.
