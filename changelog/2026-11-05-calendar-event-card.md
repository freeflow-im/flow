# Calendar: the event card, after Google Calendar's

- `[web]` The event popover is rebuilt: edit, delete, menu and close at the top; when in Google's form and the repeat in
  words (the series asked for when an occurrence carries no rule); Join with the provider's name, copy link, Join by phone.
- `[web]` `lib/conference.ts` reads Google Meet's and Zoom's dial-in blocks out of the description and hides them there;
  guests summed up and folded (organizer, then you), reminders, organizer, place with a map link, links in the description.
- `[web]` Going? Yes · No · Maybe in a band at the bottom, the answer in the calendar's colour; the card takes the focus.

## Feature

- **A clearer event card.** Click an event to see it the way Google Calendar shows it: when and how often it repeats, a
  Join button for the video call with its phone number and PIN, the guests and how many said yes, your reminders, and the
  description without the meeting boilerplate. Answer an invitation with Yes, No or Maybe at the bottom of the card.
