# Meet: video meetings on Cloudflare RealtimeKit (phase 1)

- [server] Meeting rooms in the organization's object, a RealtimeKit participant per person per room, and `/v1/config` `meet`; Live now asks RealtimeKit for each room's active session (no webhooks yet).
- [web] New Meet app at `/meet` (⌘5): start a meeting, copy a link, Live now; the room opens in its own tab with a grid, mic, camera, screen share, People and Leave.
- [desktop] Meet runs in the desktop shell as the web app.

## Feature

There is a new Meet app for video meetings. Start a meeting, send the link to a teammate in your organization, and talk face to face, with screen sharing. Meet's home shows the meetings running right now, so you can join one with a click.
