# Meet: video meetings on Cloudflare RealtimeKit (phases 1 and 2)

- [server] Meeting rooms in the organization's object, a RealtimeKit participant per person per room, and `/v1/config` `meet`; Live now asks RealtimeKit for each room's active session (no webhooks yet).
- [web] New Meet app at `/meet` (⌘5): start a meeting, copy a link, Live now; the room opens in its own tab with a grid, mic, camera, screen share, People and Leave.
- [desktop] Meet runs in the desktop shell as the web app.
- [server] Huddles are Meet rooms: Huddle makes the conversation's room and rings its people, only in rooms of 6 or fewer; RealtimeKit webhook keeps the roster right; LiveKit removed.
- [web] Chat's Huddle button and ring card open the huddle in Meet in a new tab; the LiveKit call UI is gone.
- [macos] [ios] Huddle and the ring card open the huddle in the browser; old builds fail to join until updated.
- [bridge] 0.39.0: agent voice calls removed.
- [macos] [ios] LiveKit SDK and the old in-app call bar, grid and permission alerts removed: smaller apps, faster builds.
- [server] The Node server (AWS origin) drops LiveKit: huddle join and accept answer 503 `huddles_unavailable`.

## Feature

There is a new Meet app for video meetings. Start a meeting, send the link to a teammate in your organization, and talk face to face, with screen sharing. Meet's home shows the meetings running right now, so you can join one with a click.

Huddles now open in Meet: press Huddle in a DM, a group DM or a small channel (six people or fewer) and everyone there is rung, and the call opens in its own tab with video and screen sharing. On the Mac and iPhone the huddle opens in your browser for now. Voice calls with AI agents have been removed.
