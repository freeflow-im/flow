# Agent notes in your DM reach you again

- `[server]` A personal agent's note in its DM with its person (an email waiting for approval, a parked automation) is posted through the sponsor, as its Chat replies are; it was refused ("a personal agent acts only through its sponsor").

## Feature

- **Your assistant now tells you in Chat when an email waits for your approval** (and when an automation stops), in your direct messages with it.
