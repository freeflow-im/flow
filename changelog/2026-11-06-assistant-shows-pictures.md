# The assistant shows pictures in the chat

- `[docs]` `[mail]` A reply's `![title](address)` loads as a picture when the address is one an image search in that chat returned (`image_search` joins `SHOWN_RESULTS`, so the chat has them) or one of the app's own (an attached or stored picture); any other picture shows as a link with its title. The prompt says so. Before, every picture in a reply showed as its title only.

## Feature

- **Pictures in the assistant's replies.** Ask the assistant for a photo or a logo and it shows the picture in the chat, in Docs and in Mail. Click it to open the full-size file.
