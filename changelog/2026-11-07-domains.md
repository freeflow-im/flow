# Domains: register, verify, the records to publish, the platform subdomain (platform phase 5.1)

- [server] Directory `domains` table: each organization's `<slug>.freeflow.im` (backfilled, verified, primary) and its own domains, verified by a `_freeflow.<domain>` TXT over DNS over HTTPS (`DNS_RESOLVER_URL`).
- [server] New accounts get their address on the primary domain; making a domain primary leaves existing addresses as they are (re-addressing is a later PR).
- [server] `/v1/workspaces/:id/domains` (owner/admin): list with records and a 60 s cached present/missing check, add, verify, make primary, remove (refused for the platform domain, the primary, or one with addresses on it).
- [server] The platform subdomain's MX/SPF/DMARC go through the Cloudflare DNS API (`CLOUDFLARE_DNS_TOKEN`, `CLOUDFLARE_DNS_ZONE_ID`); until 5.2's provider the records are placeholders and only logged.
- [web] A Domains tab in Manage users: add a domain, its records with Copy and a present/missing mark, Check again, Verify, Make primary, Remove.

## Feature

- **Bring your own domain.** An organization's owners and admins can add their own domain, verify it with a DNS record, and make it primary, so new members get their addresses on it. Existing members keep the addresses they have.
