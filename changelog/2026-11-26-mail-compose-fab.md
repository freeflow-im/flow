# Floating New message button over Mail's list

- `[web]` `[desktop]` Mail shows a round compose button over the list's lower-left corner wherever its folder sidebar is hidden (inside Home, phone-sized screens), styled like the assistant pill; it opens the composer.

## Feature

- **Start a new email from Home.** A round "New message" button floats over the lower-left corner of your mail list in Home; click it to write a new email.
