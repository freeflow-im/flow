# Calendar: the suite's fourth tab, reading and writing CalDAV calendars

- `[server]` A `Calendar` Durable Object per person (`/v1/calendar/*`, migration `v5`): CalDAV accounts (iCloud, Fastmail, any
  server), a complete copy of their calendars in SQLite synced by sync-collection or ctag, recurring events expanded on read.
- `[server]` Events created, moved, edited and deleted with If-Match (a change made elsewhere is `conflict`), recurring scopes
  (this, this and following, all), RSVP and free/busy; the alarm syncs every minute while Calendar is in use.
- `[qa]` `packages/edge/test/e2e-calendar.mjs` against Radicale in Docker; query-plan tests on 10 000 events.
