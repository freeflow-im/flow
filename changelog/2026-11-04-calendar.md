# Calendar: the suite's fourth tab, reading and writing CalDAV calendars

- `[server]` A `Calendar` Durable Object per person (`/v1/calendar/*`, migration `v5`): CalDAV accounts (iCloud, Fastmail, any
  server), a complete copy of their calendars in SQLite synced by sync-collection or ctag, recurring events expanded on read.
- `[server]` Events created, moved, edited and deleted with If-Match (a change made elsewhere is `conflict`), recurring scopes
  (this, this and following, all), RSVP and free/busy; the alarm syncs every minute while Calendar is in use.
- `[web]` `packages/calendar` at `/calendar` on FullCalendar: day, week, month and agenda, the calendars overlaid with their
  colours, quick create, the edit dialog (repeat, guests, reminders, zone), drag to move and resize, this / following / all.
- `[web]` The Calendar tab is live in the suite bar of Chat, Mail, Docs and Calendar (⌘4); `/calendar` served by the Worker.
- `[server]` `[web]` Google Calendar through its CalDAV endpoint with OAuth: sign in with Google (auth-code flow on the
  Worker, the refresh token sealed), Reconnect when Google stops giving access.
- `[qa]` `packages/edge/test/e2e-calendar.mjs` against Radicale in Docker; query-plan tests on 10 000 events.

## Feature

- **Calendar.** Connect your Google Calendar (sign in with Google), iCloud, Fastmail or other CalDAV calendar and see all your calendars together by day, week,
  month or as an agenda, each in its own colour. Click or drag on the calendar to add an event, drag events to move or
  resize them, and open one to see who is coming, join its video call or answer an invitation.
- **Repeating events.** Make an event repeat daily, weekly, on weekdays, monthly or yearly, or on a rule of your own; when
  you change or delete one, choose this event, this and following, or all of them.
- **Changes everywhere.** Events you add or change elsewhere show up within a minute, and a change that crosses one made on
  another device is never lost: Calendar shows you the latest instead.
