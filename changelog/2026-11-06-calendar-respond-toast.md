# Calendar: answering an invitation says so in a toast

- `[web]` The event card's Yes · Maybe · No shows the answer at once and a toast "Updating…" while it is sent, then "Accepted", "Declined" or "Tentatively accepted"; a failure puts the card back and says why (it used to be swallowed).

## Feature

- **Answering an invitation in Calendar is instant.** Your answer shows on the event right away, a note at the bottom says when it is saved, and if it could not be saved the event goes back to how it was and you are told why.
