# Pictures shown in a message are listed under it

- `[mail]` The opened message lists the pictures its HTML shows by cid: among its attachments, so a flyer pasted into the body can be previewed and saved (as Gmail lists it). The thread list's row still shows only real files, so signature logos do not clutter it.

## Feature

- **Save a picture that is part of a message.** A picture pasted into an email (a flyer, a screenshot) now appears under the message beside its other attachments, where you can preview and download it.
