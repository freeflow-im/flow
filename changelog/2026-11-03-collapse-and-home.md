# The folder panel folds away, and the FreeFlow logo goes home

- `[docs]` The Docs folder panel has a button at its top right that folds it to a narrow rail of icons (Recents, Favorites, Organization files, My files), giving the page its room; the button at the rail's top brings it back. Remembered in this browser.
- `[web]` `[docs]` Clicking the FreeFlow logo in the suite bar goes to the home of the app you are in: Docs' file list, Chat's #general, Mail's inbox.
