# Mail: invite card for your own event says you're the organizer

- `[web]` Mail's invite card no longer shows "Not answered yet" and Yes · No · Maybe on an event the person organizes
  (e.g. a booking on their own calendar); it says "You're the organizer".

## Feature

- **Bookings on your own calendar read correctly in Mail.** The invite card on an event you organize now says
  you're the organizer, not "Not answered yet".
