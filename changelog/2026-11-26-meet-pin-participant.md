# Meet: pin a participant

- `[web]` `[desktop]` A pin button on each Meet tile and shared screen puts that view on the stage, with every other view in a strip of small squares. Unpin goes back to the grid.
- `[web]` `[desktop]` A pin follows someone who rejoins and is dropped when they leave. Pinning applies only to your own view.

## Feature

- **Pin someone in a meeting.** Hover over a person's tile, or a shared screen, and choose the pin to make it fill most of the meeting. Everyone else shows as small squares below it. Choose the pin again to unpin. Only your own view changes.
