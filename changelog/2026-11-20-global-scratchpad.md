# Global scratchpad beside the bell

- `[web]` `[desktop]` A scratchpad button beside the suite bar's bell opens the user's "Global Scratchpad" whiteboard in a movable, resizable popup over any app (Taskboard task 16).
- `[server]` `POST /api/whiteboards/scratchpad` finds or makes that whiteboard at the top of My files (found by name: renamed or moved, it is an ordinary board).
- `[web]` Docs' framed `/docs/scratchpad` page: the board and its toolbar only.

## Feature

- **A scratchpad, one click away.** The new scratchpad button next to the notifications bell opens your Global Scratchpad — a whiteboard kept in My files in Docs — in a small window over whatever you are doing. Jot a note, sketch, or paste a picture, then close it; drag its title bar to move it and its corner to resize it.
