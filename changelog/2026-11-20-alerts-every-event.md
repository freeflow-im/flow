# Meeting alerts for every event

- `[web]` `[desktop]` Meeting alerts (card and desktop notification) now cover every timed event on your shown calendars that is not cancelled or declined: events with no guests and unanswered invitations too. Before, only accepted meetings or ones you organized with guests or a call.

## Feature

- **Reminders for all your events.** The 2-minute reminder now comes for every event on your calendar, not only meetings you accepted. Events you declined and all-day events are still skipped.
